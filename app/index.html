    The road to the Platinum

    Seven stages, in order, no skipping. About 200 hours from the first mission to the last pop — roughly 60–80 for the story, about 100 for the 100% checklist, and about 10 for the strangers and the cleanup. One playthrough is enough, and there are no online trophies.
